Class Refund Policy

We understand that schedules change. If you register and cannot attend a class, please email us within the first week of class commencement and request a refund. We will refund the cost after deducting a $20 processing fee. Any refunds requested after the first week of class commencement are no longer provided. Please reach out with any questions.

Family Discount:
A $10 discount per child is available for families registering multiple children. If you are registering more than one child from your family, please select Yes for each child's registration. Each registered child will receive a $10 discount.
