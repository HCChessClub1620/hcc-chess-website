Weekly Class Schedules

No matter your skill level, there is always something happening at Hamilton Chess Club. Our weekly chess programming offers a mix of lessons, tournaments designed to help players of all levels sharpen their skills, challenge themselves, and connect with the chess community. Whether you are looking to improve your game, test your strategy, or gain insights from our coach, our weekly events provide the perfect opportunity to grow as a chess player.

Upcoming Tournaments

Hamilton Chess Club Tournaments

Practice matches and club tournaments are planned throughout the season. Internal match events are conducted regularly to prepare students for rated competition, strengthen decision-making under pressure, and build confidence in a tournament-like environment. Upcoming dates and details will be announced soon.

Regional Scholastic Tournaments - WSCF

Students participate as a group representing Hamilton Chess Club in WSCF scholastic tournaments. Training milestones, tournament readiness checks, and coach guidance help students transition from classroom practice to competitive play.

Upcoming Tournaments - Wisconsin Scholastic Chess Federation

National-Level Tournaments - USCF

Students are encouraged to participate in USCF-rated tournaments to gain experience against players from different clubs and regions. These events provide opportunities to earn and improve official ratings, test tournament skills, track competitive progress, and prepare for higher-level scholastic and national competitions.
