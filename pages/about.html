Hamilton Chess Club - Our Journey

Our Story

Hamilton Chess Club was founded in September 2025 by Balaji Narayanan and Deepak Gupta with a mission to provide structured chess coaching for kids and students across Menomonee Falls, Pewaukee, Sussex, Waukesha, Franklin, Milwaukee, Brookfield, and surrounding Wisconsin communities.

What began as a growing interest among students in local schools quickly revealed a larger gap—many young players wanted to learn chess seriously and compete in tournaments, but lacked access to consistent chess coaching, structured curriculum, and organized competitive training programs in their area.

To address this need, Hamilton Chess Club was created as a local chess training academy and community-based chess club, offering professional guidance, beginner-to-advanced instruction, and a supportive learning environment where students can build strong chess fundamentals and improve steadily over time.

Our coaching approach focuses on both skill development and competitive readiness, helping students participate confidently in USCF-rated and WSCF-rated chess tournaments while building long-term improvement through practice, strategy, and mentorship.

Proudly built as a Parent–Student Volunteering Initiative, Hamilton Chess Club has grown into a trusted local chess community serving families across Southeast Wisconsin. We continue to expand access to quality chess education in areas where structured coaching is limited, helping students grow both on and off the chessboard.

Policies & Commitments

What We Stand For

These statements guide how we coach, welcome families, and support students in a safe and respectful chess environment.

01

Mission

Build confident chess players through structured coaching, tournament preparation, and positive mentorship.

Mission

Hamilton Chess Club exists to help students grow as chess players and as people. Our mission is to develop essential life skills through chess, including critical thinking, discipline, patience, focus, problem-solving, confidence, and resilience, while giving every student a clear path to improvement. It is also the mission of Hamilton Chess Club to maintain a formal program of instruction to teach the game of chess and to promote and support its educational program through community outreach and local and national partnerships to increase awareness of the educational value of chess.

  • Structured instruction for beginners through advanced students
  • Preparation for club play and tournament competition
  • A supportive environment that builds confidence
02

Non-Discrimination

Every student and family is welcome here.

Non-Discrimination Policy

Hamilton Chess Club provides access to coaching, events, and club participation without discrimination based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, or any other protected status.

We are committed to treating every student and family with fairness, dignity, and respect.

03

Safeguarding

Professional boundaries and age-appropriate interactions are always required.

Policy on Relationships and Interaction With Minors

All coaches, volunteers, and representatives are expected to maintain professional, respectful boundaries with minors at all times. Interactions must remain appropriate, instruction-focused, and consistent with student safety and well-being, and are strictly prohibited from engaging in sexual misconduct, hazing, and harassment.

  • Communication stays club-related and age-appropriate
  • Interactions with minors should be supervised whenever practical
  • No inappropriate personal, romantic, or exploitative relationships are permitted
